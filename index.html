How PlasticTrack Works

A simple three-step approach to understand and improve plastic-use behaviour.

1. 📋 Track

Answer a short survey about your everyday plastic-use habits and get your personal behaviour score.

2. 🌱 Reduce

Follow simple actions during the 7-day challenge, such as using reusable bottles and avoiding plastic bags.

3. 📊 Measure

Take the survey again and compare your before and after scores to understand your behaviour change.

Why PlasticTrack?

The goal is not just awareness. It is measurable behaviour change.

🎯 Personal Awareness

Understand which everyday habits contribute to unnecessary plastic usage.

📈 Measure Progress

Compare your behaviour before and after the challenge using a simple Plastic Usage Behaviour Score.

🤝 Community Impact

Community responses can be analysed to understand overall behaviour change and encourage sustainable habits.
