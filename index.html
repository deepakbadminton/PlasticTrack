ABOUT THE PROJECT

A small digital solution for a real community problem

PlasticTrack was developed as a VTU Community Project to study everyday plastic-use behaviour and encourage practical reduction habits.

🔎

Problem

Everyday use of single-use plastic items can contribute to unnecessary plastic waste.

💡

Approach

Collect a baseline response, conduct a simple awareness and habit-building activity, then measure the behaviour again.

📈

Expected Outcome

Identify whether participants report lower plastic-use behaviour after the intervention.
